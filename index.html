What you’ll leave with

This is a practical introduction—not an exam course, and not a promise to turn anyone into a performer in four weeks.

1

Know the instrument

Recognise the dayan and bayan, sit comfortably, and understand how the sounds are made.

2

Make first sounds

Explore foundational bols and learn how spoken rhythm connects to the hands.

3

Feel a taal

Clap, count, and play a simple beginner rhythm with steadiness and enjoyment.

4

Practise simply

Leave with a ten-minute home practice routine that feels achievable between sessions.

5

Decide your next step

Understand what longer-term tabla learning looks like and whether you want to continue.

6

Enjoy the process

Experience an Indian classical rhythm tradition in a relaxed, welcoming group.

The Tablafy approach

Live guidance first. Simple practice after.

A screen cannot correct posture, hear a stroke, or make a beginner feel at ease. The workshop is built around live teaching; enrolled learners receive a focused practice companion after class.

1

Learn together

Meet the instrument and learn a small idea at a time in a supportive group.

2

Practise at home

Use only the materials relevant to that week—no overwhelming course library.

3

Build from there

At the end, decide whether to continue with further study or enjoy what you have learned.
